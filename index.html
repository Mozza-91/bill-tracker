Back up your data

Your data is saved on this phone only, so make a backup copy now and then. Tap Download backup file and keep it somewhere safe, such as Google Drive, or email it to yourself. To restore on this or a new phone, tap Restore from file and choose that file. You can also tap Show backup to copy the text, then paste it into the box and tap Restore. Restoring replaces what is in the app now.
