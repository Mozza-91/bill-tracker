Add a bill

A bill repeats every month from the month you add it and counts as paid once its due date has passed. Tap Edit to change a bill, either from this month onwards or for this month only, such as a variable energy bill. The × stops a bill from the month you are viewing and keeps your past months.

Back up your data

Your data is stored only on this phone, so back it up now and then. Download a backup file and keep it somewhere safe, such as Google Drive, or copy the text instead. To restore, choose the file, or paste the text into the box and tap Restore. Restoring replaces what is in the app now. Moving from the Claude version? Paste its backup text here.
